🧠 Data & Setup Rules

DIRECT CATALYST = fresh company-specific headline with a recognizable event. Market/sector articles alone do not qualify.
VOLUME BURST = recent 25-minute average volume divided by the preceding intraday reference window. It is deliberately labeled as a burst signal, not a true time-of-day normalized RVOL.
OPTIONS FLOW = near-money same-day call/put volume ratio. Stock direction is never used as a substitute for options flow.
CONFIRMED WATCH = catalyst + underlying momentum/acceleration + qualifying contract. It is still a watch state, not an automatic entry.
SECOND-WAVE = catalyst/acceleration developing after the first pass; useful for repricing and re-entry scans.
TRIGGER / INVALIDATION = mechanical levels derived from recent 5-minute price action and VWAP. Verify live before acting.
