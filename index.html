📋 EXECUTION RULES

1. Find the catalyst.
Earnings, guidance, analyst action, FDA/news, contracts, M&A, major company news or another identifiable reason for movement.
2. Confirm momentum.
Price movement should be supported by accelerating volume and, ideally, continuation rather than a single isolated spike.
3. Confirm the options.
Prefer liquid contracts, reasonable spreads, meaningful volume/OI, near-ATM positioning and useful delta/gamma.
4. Re-scan rejected names.
A stock rejected earlier can become actionable if price, volume or options activity materially accelerates.
5. ENTER-READY requires live confirmation.
The dashboard can identify a setup, but delayed CBOE data does not independently authorize an entry.
