# IWM staging comparison, version 1

Frozen specification: November 19, 2026. Effective only for staging packets first captured after deployment. Original packet clocks determine inclusion. Earlier exports are retrospective and must never be pooled as prospective observations.

Capture every IWM stock state from each fresh OPEN staging scan, including WATCH and unqualified states. Keep original bar, news, pattern, volume, trend and eligibility fields. Missing IWM is an explicit coverage gap. Compare existing qualified states against unqualified controls within session and time of day. Use the existing pipeline and budget; change no production score, gate, contract budget or entry rule.

Hypothesis: aligned downward multi-timeframe trend and recurring bearish patterns may provide useful context even when IWM has no company-specific catalyst. Record momentum, volume acceleration, research readiness and rejection reasons before reviewing subsequent observations. Do not label missing news as no catalyst.

First review after five complete prospective sessions: report coverage, missing clocks, qualification counts and stock-only subsequent observed moves at 15/30/60 minutes. Match only completed later bars from the same session, preserve original availability and report missing horizons. Session is the unit of comparison; correlated scans are not independent trades. Five sessions is an exploratory checkpoint, not proof of profitability. An outcome collector and reviewer must validate these horizons before any performance claim; current packets freeze inputs only.

Option results require separately timestamped, exact-contract entry and exit observations with spread/fees. Delayed payload timestamps are not quote timestamps. No stock movement can establish an option return. No automated tuning or promotion. Any later hypothesis/spec change starts a new cohort; production promotion needs a reviewed multi-session report, quote integrity and risk-gate checks.
