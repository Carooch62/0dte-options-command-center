## Important weighting clarification

The agreed **40% catalysts/news, 38% patterns/trends, 10% volume/momentum, 7% options quality, 5% risk/reward** is a reference allocation for the proposed composite comparison. It is **not an already implemented production composite score**.

Production `scanner.py` computes a raw stock score from absolute day/15-minute/5-minute moves and excess volume burst/acceleration. News attaches a catalyst classification; final ordering also prioritizes execution state, direct catalyst, market alignment and momentum. These priorities do not mathematically implement a normalized 40/38/10/7/5 allocation. Staging's rubric documentation already says the allocation is reference-only; a separate staging document incorrectly called it fixed production weights and is corrected by this review.

Current version 2 shadow allocations range from news 40–42.5% and patterns 38–35.5%; historical version 1 outputs retain their original ranges. No production score, threshold, ranking or eligibility is changed by this QC release. Promotion still requires complete point-in-time component evidence, prospective outcomes and chronological validation. An allocation proposal is not a probability or evidence of profitability.

## Source and calculation checks

- Production and staging snapshots have 208 and 209 unique ticker rows respectively. Both snapshots retain their original clocks; their inputs and collection times differ and must not be treated as a same-input performance comparison.
- The production current-quote batch contains 13 unique contracts, at most two per ticker, all with research asks above zero and at most $0.30. Its scan ID and generation timestamp match the underlying dashboard snapshot. These are research asks, not verified current execution prices.
- Checked all 236 production and 269 staging retained eligible contracts against the applicable quote validity, verified expiry, delta, near-ATM, direction, minimum-volume and **usable-spread** gates. The tighter preferred spread is a separate display/shortlist filter; it must not be confused with the broader retained eligibility predicate.
- No inconsistent `CONFIRMED` state was observed in the reviewed snapshots. The current production snapshot is after hours and must not be presented as a fresh live signal. The production health record discloses degraded coverage, including one option-source failure; this is not a full-universe coverage guarantee.
- Staging retains seven evidence packets, six without prospective pattern tracking, and zero observed forming-to-confirmation transitions in the reviewed prospective ledger. All 75 retained public outcome entries are withheld. These facts do not establish predictive improvement or readiness to activate adaptive ranking.
