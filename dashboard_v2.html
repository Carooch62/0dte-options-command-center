GitHub Actions

Auto-check downloads the latest published results. Cloudflare requests a scan every five minutes during regular weekday hours; GitHub may queue or delay execution. Market bars and option quotes have their own timestamps.

Candidate filters

Trend filters cover intraday movement and completed daily bars. Stale intraday data is unavailable for filtering. Alignment compares all three timeframes with the scan direction, regardless of the selected timeframe. Unavailable or stale daily context never counts as rising, falling or aligned. These filters do not confirm an entry. Badges and state counts show the last timestamped setup, not a live entry signal. Each card keeps its own update time. Filters use the full fetched eligible chain. Names above your preferred price remain available for later scans. Call/put volume balance does not establish buying pressure. TRIGGERED means the stock crossed a level; it is not an option entry.

Prospective option observations

Signal-time asks are compared with later observed bids. These are quoted observations, not fills. Unknown quote timing is labeled and fees are excluded. No win rate is inferred from stock movement.

Your trade limits

Optional reminders based on the trades you record here. Blank limits are unset. They do not place or close orders.

Position tracker & trade journal

Saved on this browser. Export a backup to keep it across devices. Leave exit blank for an open trade. Total fees include entry and exit costs. Review levels are reminders, not automatic orders. For re-entries use the same contract description and record a fresh confirmation after the previous exit. Quotes are updated manually; this does not connect to your brokerage.
