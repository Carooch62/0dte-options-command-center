🧩 Rules We Added

EXECUTION STATES = PASS → SECOND-WAVE → WATCH → TRIGGERED → CONFIRMED, with DECAYING as a protective state when momentum fades after a trigger/confirmation.
KEY LEVEL ENGINE = support, resistance, trigger, invalidation and two mechanical extension levels derived from the current 5-minute/VWAP structure.
MOMENTUM DECAY = 5M movement and volume acceleration are compared with the prior snapshot.
CHASE RISK = combines 5M displacement, distance from VWAP and distance from the trigger.
CONTRACT FIT = BALANCED near-ATM, AGGRESSIVE moderate-OTM, or DEEP OTM/high leverage. Distance to strike is displayed explicitly.
MARKET REGIME = core liquid ETFs provide context only; individual stock confirmation remains primary.
